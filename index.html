About Nirmaviyo

A studio that builds systems, not one-off pages.

Nirmaviyo is a digital services studio — websites, email marketing, and automation, built as one connected system instead of three disconnected deliverables.

Why Nirmaviyo

Most agencies hand you pieces. We hand you a system.

Too many businesses end up with a website built by one person, emails written by another, and no automation connecting the two — so leads fall through the gap between them. Nirmaviyo exists to close that gap: one team, one system, built to keep working after launch day.

How we work

Strategy first, then build

Every project starts with understanding your business and where visitors are dropping off — not with a template. We plan the system, design and build it, then stay on to refine it once real traffic hits.

AG

Anshika Gupta

Founder & CEO, Nirmaviyo

Nirmaviyo started from a simple frustration: watching businesses treat their website, their emails, and their automation as three separate projects — built by three different people who never talk to each other.

I build all three as one system from day one, so nothing gets lost between a visitor's first click and their first email.

What we believe

The principles behind every build

01

Strategy before execution — we plan the system before we design a single screen.

02

Design should have a purpose — every element earns its place or it doesn't ship.

03

Websites should convert, not just look good — a beautiful site that doesn't turn visitors into customers isn't finished.

04

Communication should feel human — automated doesn't have to mean robotic.
